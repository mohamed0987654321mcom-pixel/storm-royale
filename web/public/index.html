          COMMUNITY RULES

          Storm Royale is for everyone, so the same strict rules apply to all players, adults too.

          1. Be kind. No put-downs, bullying, hate or harassment, not even "as a joke".
          2. No swearing, including hidden or misspelled swears.
          3. Nothing sexual and no dating or romance talk, ever.
          4. Never share or ask for personal info: real name, age, birthday, where you live, school, photos, phone or email.
          5. Stay here: no other apps, socials or usernames, and no "dm me".
          6. No links, scams, "free Robux", account trading or spam.
          7. Trying to sneak past the filter counts as breaking the rule.
          8. If someone makes you uncomfortable: block them, report them, and tell a parent or adult you trust.

          IF YOU BREAK THE RULES

          Your message is hidden. Small slips are a warning (3 warnings in an hour = a strike). Each strike mutes you for longer: 15 min, 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days, then a 7-day ban. Grooming or sexual messages mean an instant ban. If several players report you, you're muted while a moderator takes a look.

          Players under 13 need a parent's permission, only play with friends their parents approved, use quick chat (preset phrases), and only get typing or voice if a verified parent turns it on. Chat and voice are checked by an AI moderator (Claude). Your browser turns your speech into text for this; audio is never recorded. Teens (13–17) and adults (18+) are kept apart: separate lobbies, and they can never be in the same party.
