How Storm Royale keeps kids safe
  • Kids only play with friends you approve. Both children's parents must say yes before they can party up. There are no open lobbies with strangers.
  • Kids never mix with teens or adults. Kids, teens and adults are kept in separate groups everywhere on the site.
  • Chat and voice are off until a verified parent turns them on. When on, an AI moderator (Claude) checks every message, and voice is turned into text in your child's browser and checked the same way. Audio is never recorded.
  • What we store: your child's display name, birth date and email, your email, their approved friends and settings, and reports if something goes wrong. Messages are sent to our AI moderation provider (Anthropic) only so they can be checked. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.
  • You're in control: you can turn things off, remove friends, or delete the account and its data at any time.
